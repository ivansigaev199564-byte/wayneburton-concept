Three directions for the new website

Same content and features in each: your albums with song previews, Walk With Me in every language, videos, sheet music and a search by theme. Only the look and feel are different. Pick the one that feels most like you, or mix parts of them.

1. Immersive

Your music video plays full screen behind your name. Scrolling moves the story: letters fly apart, Walk With Me changes language, albums slide sideways.

  • Full-screen video
  • Scroll animation
  • Dark, cinematic
Open concept 1
2. Music app

Feels like Apple Music or Spotify. Tiles for everything, colors follow the album you’re looking at, and a player that stays with you on every page.

  • App-like
  • Always-on player
  • Dark glass
Open concept 2
3. Poster

Bright, bold and joyful, like a concert poster. Big colors and giant type, made to catch the eye of the youth who sing your theme songs.

  • Bright colors
  • Giant type
  • Youthful
